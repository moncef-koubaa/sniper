# CV rules (FR + EN)

Strength: **always** (≥ 80 % of the 13 CVs) · **usually** (50–80 %) · **variable** (depends on target/language) ·
**hard** (imposed by Nermine, overrides samples). Examples cite `profile/me.md` source IDs.

## Layout and structure

- **CV-01 One page, LaTeX, same template.** Build from `resumes/library/_template/` or a library `source.tex`;
  never re-type in Word/HTML. *always* (13/13 one page, 13/13 pdfTeX).
- **CV-02 Section order:** FORMATION → EXPÉRIENCE PROFESSIONNELLE → (EXPÉRIENCE COMPLÉMENTAIRE) → PROJETS
  ACADÉMIQUES → COMPÉTENCES TECHNIQUES → ACTIVITÉS EXTRASCOLAIRES → LANGUES ET CERTIFICATS. EN: EDUCATION →
  PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE → (ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCE) → ACADEMIC PROJECTS → TECHNICAL SKILLS → EXTRACURRICULAR
  ACTIVITIES → LANGUAGES (AND CERTIFICATES). *always* (13/13).
- **CV-03 Header:** name centred, large (FR "Moumen Nermine", EN "Nermine Moumen"); one title line ("Étudiante en
  dernière année du cycle ingénieur en Chimie Industrielle" / "Final-year Industrial Chemistry Engineering
  Student"); then `email · +21627040414 · linkedin.com/in/nermine-moumen`. No photo, address, age. *always*.
  Ex.: CV01 widens the title to "… en Chimie – Génie des procédés, énergie et environnement" for process roles.
- **CV-04 Education = INSAT line + 3-line summary paragraph** (no separate "Profil" section):
  « Étudiante en Chimie Industrielle, spécialité Procédés et Environnement, avec une formation solide en A, B et C,
  complétée par [des bases en] D et E. Orientée vers / Intéressée par F. À la recherche d'un PFE de 6 mois à partir
  de février 2027. » *always* (13/13). EN: "Industrial Chemistry student, specializing in Process and Environmental
  Engineering, with a solid training in A, B and C, complemented by … Interested in … Seeking a 6-month final-year
  internship." Ex.: CV05, CV03. Counter-ex.: old EN CVs (CV10, CV11, CV13) put the summary in a bullet — don't.
- **CV-05 The summary is the main tailoring surface:** A–C = the 3 courses closest to the target; "Orientée vers…"
  = the target's activity in her words; last sentence may name the target field ("À la recherche d'un PFE en
  décarbonation et efficacité énergétique de l'industrie", CV06). *always tailored*.
- **CV-06 Entry heading:** bold `Company - Stage en <domaine>` (FR, hyphen) / `Company – <Domain> Internship` (EN, en
  dash), dates right-aligned `MM/YYYY–MM/YYYY`; INSAT `2022–Présent` / `2022–Present`. *always*.

## Bullets

- **CV-07 Anatomy:** action → method/tool → object → quantified result. FR uses a **nominal action** (Modélisation,
  Optimisation, Validation, Analyse, Synthèse, Étude, Réalisation, Préparation, Formation aux…), EN newest uses a
  **past-tense verb** (Modeled, Implemented, Optimized, Extracted, Performed, Prepared). *always* (FR 9/9 nominal;
  EN: 4/4 newest-family verbs; CV03 keeps FR nominal style when translated → acceptable but prefer verbs).
  Ex.: « Optimisation du maillage via une analyse des données réduisant l'erreur RMSE de 202,13 mV à 40,5 mV. » (CV01)
  · "Optimized the mesh and diffusion coefficient values, reducing the RMSE error from 202.13 mV to 40.5 mV." (CV02)
- **CV-08 Length:** 10–20 words, never more than 2 printed lines (≈ 30 words). Measured average 12–16.5, max 34
  (CV10 has one 43-word bullet: avoid). *always*.
- **CV-09 Impersonal:** no "je/I/my". *always* (13/13).
- **CV-10 Bold keywords inside bullets** — tools, techniques, standards, key results matching the target
  (**Li-ion**, **FVM**, **RMSE de 202,13 mV à 40,5 mV**, **ICP-OES**, **ISO 17025**). 2–4 bolds per bullet max. *always*.
- **CV-11 Quantify with her exact numbers** (202,13 → 40,5 mV or "de 80 %", 96 %/97,14 %, 85 %/57 % en 20 min,
  0,584 V, 1,40 L/h, 99,95 % mol, 0,50 L/j à 64,52 % CH4, R² = 0,9877, 240 min). ≈ 40 % of bullets carry a number;
  every experience/project that has a number in me.md shows it. *usually*.
- **CV-12 Number format follows language:** FR decimal comma and space before % (`97,14 %`, `85 %`), EN point and no
  space (`97.14%`). *usually* (FR CVs sometimes write `85%` — fix it).
- **CV-13 End bullets with a period** — consistently, not mixed. *variable in samples* (CV02/CV04 mix) → apply always.

## Content blocks

- **CV-14 Experience:** reverse chronological; SEGULA 3–4 bullets, Control Union 2, AGIL 1 (optional), ETAP 2–3.
  *usually*. AGIL is the first entry dropped for space (present in 3/13).
- **CV-15 Teaching is separate:** Cascade lives in "EXPÉRIENCE COMPLÉMENTAIRE / ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCE", one bullet,
  never inside professional experience; dropped when space is needed (7/13 keep it). *always separate*.
- **CV-16 3–4 academic projects, 2–3 bullets each, most relevant first.** *always* (3–4 in 13/13).
- **CV-17 Exact project titles from me.md §4, in bold, tool in the title when it has one** ("… sous Aspen HYSYS V14").
  *hard* (+ always in samples, with older variants now retired).
- **CV-18 Skills block, one line per group:** Techniques de Laboratoire · Simulation · Programmation · Données &
  Bureautique · Normes (EN: Lab Techniques · Simulation · Programming · Data & Office · Standards). The group
  closest to the target comes first (process/energy: Simulation first, CV01/CV06/CV10; lab/materials: Techniques
  first). *variable (target)*.
- **CV-19 Lab techniques written as families with parentheses**: Chromatographie (HPLC, GC, CCM), Spectrométrie
  (MS, ICP-OES), Spectroscopie (RMN, FTIR, UV-Vis), Diffraction (XRD), Microscopie (MEB, MET), Électrochimie
  (Voltamétrie cyclique, polarographie). *always*.
- **CV-20 Extracurriculars, one line per organisation:** "IEEE INSAT — Chef d'organisation de TRSYP 3.0 ; Responsable
  des activités sociales – MENASYP ; Présidente de l'EMBS." / "THEATRO INSAT — Membre de l'équipe de danse." *always*.
- **CV-21 Last block:** Langues (Anglais (Avancé), Français (Bilingue), Arabe (Natif)) | Certificats (Aspen HYSYS –
  MATLAB), two columns. *always*.

## Tailoring rules (learned from the differences between versions)

- **TL-01 Swap projects by target domain** (seen across 13 versions):
  process/energy → P3, P2, P1, P4 · electrochemistry → P5, P1, P6, P7 · materials/green chem → P6, P7, P3, P4 ·
  water → P5, P9, P6 · waste/circular → P10, P11, P3 · analytical → P8, P6, P4 · hydrogen → P1 first.
- **TL-02 Promote the bullet that matches the target and give it the bold keywords** (CV01 promotes the reactor
  geometry for process roles; CV02 gives P5 three bullets for electrochemistry).
- **TL-03 Rewrite internship bullets toward the target without new facts** (CV06 rewrites Control Union toward
  documentation/traceability; CV07 adds the ETAP water-treatment bullet).
- **TL-04 Absolute vs relative results:** technical/modelling targets → "de 202,13 mV à 40,5 mV"; general or
  non-modelling targets → "réduisant l'erreur de prédiction de plus de 80 %".
- **TL-05 Add the target's keywords only where a me.md fact supports them** (skills line, summary, bolds).
- **TL-06 Space recovery order:** drop AGIL → drop Cascade → cut 4th project → shorten skills to 2 lines.

## Never

- **NV-01** Never a fact, number, tool or title absent from `profile/me.md`.
- **NV-02** Never "hands-on", "maîtrise", "expert" for a technique in me.md §5c; never reuse the CL02/CL03 overclaims.
- **NV-03** No grades/rank (none documented), no photo, age, address, marital status.
- **NV-04** No first person, no soft-skill bullets ("rigoureuse…") on the CV — soft skills belong to the letter.
- **NV-05** No orthographic slips she has made before: "matériaux fonctionnel(s)", "manger" for "manager",
  "électrochim-ique" hyphenation breaks (CV04) — keep words unhyphenated in short lines where possible.
