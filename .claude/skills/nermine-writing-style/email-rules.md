# Email rules (spontaneous applications and offer emails)

No email samples yet (`writing-samples/emails/` is empty): these rules come from Nermine's hard rules plus her
letter style. Strength: **hard** unless stated. `/learn-style` will refine them from real emails.

- **EM-01 Length 120–180 words** (body, without signature). Check with `wc -w`.
- **EM-02 Subject line pattern:**
  FR spontané « Candidature spontanée – Stage de fin d'études (6 mois, dès février 2027) – Génie des procédés » ;
  FR offre « Candidature – <intitulé exact> – Nermine Moumen » ;
  EN spontaneous "Final-year internship request (6 months from February 2027) – Process engineering – Nermine Moumen" ;
  EN offer "Application – <exact offer title> – Nermine Moumen". Swap "Génie des procédés / Process engineering" for
  the target domain (Électrochimie / Battery modelling / Hydrogen / LCA …). ≤ 90 characters.
- **EM-03 Salutation = real name** (same rule as CL-04). Generic only when no name exists → `GENERIC SALUTATION`.
- **EM-04 Opener (1 sentence):** who I am, in the letter's ¶1 words, shortened: « Étudiante en dernière année
  d'ingénieur en Chimie Industrielle (Procédés et Environnement) à l'INSAT, Tunisie, … » / "I am a final-year
  Industrial Chemical Engineering student at INSAT (Tunisia), specializing in Process and Environment."
- **EM-05 Specific hook (1 sentence):** one concrete thing about the company/team (a project, a site, a publication,
  a product, a press release) with why it matters to me. Never generic praise.
- **EM-06 One proof point (1–2 sentences):** the single most relevant internship/project from me.md, with tool and
  number (e.g. SEGULA SPM model, RMSE 202,13 → 40,5 mV).
- **EM-07 Clear request (1 sentence):** « Je souhaiterais savoir s'il serait possible d'effectuer mon stage de fin
  d'études au sein de votre équipe, à partir du 1er février 2027, pour 6 mois (3 à 9 mois possibles), ou, à défaut,
  être orientée vers la personne appropriée. » / "Would your team consider hosting my final-year internship from
  1 February 2027 for 6 months (3 to 9 possible) — or could you point me to the right person?"
- **EM-08 Attachment line:** « Vous trouverez mon CV en pièce jointe. » / "My CV is attached." (+ letter if one exists).
- **EM-09 Closing:** FR « Je vous remercie par avance pour votre attention. Bien cordialement, » / EN "Thank you for
  your time. Best regards," then signature block: Nermine Moumen · Élève-ingénieure / Engineering student, INSAT ·
  nermine.moumen@insat.ucar.tn · +216 27 040 414 · linkedin.com/in/nermine-moumen.
- **EM-10 Plain text, no bullet lists, no bold, ≤ 5 short paragraphs.**
- **EM-11 Language** = company's working language (CLAUDE.md), feminine forms in FR.
- **EM-12 Never** mention a gap or a missing skill (NV-L6); never a §5c item presented as hands-on; never a fact not in me.md; never "I hope this email finds you well".
