Friendly Setup and Usage 🧸

How to set up your internship helper on any computer, what it needs to work, and the commands you use, in easy steps.

Your helper is Claude, a computer assistant. Each time you start it with a command, it looks for internships, writes your letters, sends applications and reads your emails. You only answer the important questions.

This guide is for setting it up on a new or different computer. Take your time. Do one step, check it worked, then do the next one. About 1 to 2 hours in total, mostly waiting.

The magic folder 📁

Everything comes in one folder, sniper. Copy it to the new computer (for example into Desktop\pfe workflow) and open 00_START_HERE.md. The setup then builds, next to it:

🏠campaignThe house where the helper lives and works: your list of internships, your letters, your reports. You always open Claude here.
📦sniperThe toolbox: the instructions for Claude, the setup message, the guides. It holds nothing personal. It is used once: at the end of the setup it deletes itself for good.

Also next to them: interviews (one folder per interview), my documents (your CV, reports, letters) and guides.

What you have on the new computerWhat Claude does
📦 a new sniper and 🏠 campaignMoves in and carries on exactly where you were.
📦 sniper onlyBuilds a brand-new campaign. It asks you a few questions about yourself, then for your CV, reports and letters.
Never delete the campaign folder: it is your whole campaign. Keep a copy of it (OneDrive or a USB stick) when you change computers.

Claude Code in simple words 💬

🤖 Claude Code is Claude inside a window on your computer called PowerShell. You type in normal words, press Enter, and Claude does the work: it reads and writes files, opens Chrome, prepares emails. Before anything big, it asks you first. You install it in step 5 of Part 1.

1️⃣ Open PowerShell 🪟

Click the Start menu, type PowerShell, press Enter. A blue or black window opens with a blinking line: that is where you type.

💡 Shortcut: open the folder you want in File Explorer, click the address bar at the top, type powershell and press Enter. PowerShell opens already inside that folder.

2️⃣ Go to the right folder 📂

Claude works in the folder where you start it. To go into a folder, type cd, a space, then the folder path between quotes (the quotes are needed because the names have spaces):

type in PowerShellcd "{{WORKSPACE}}"

🔄 The paths in this guide are examples. When Claude sets up the campaign on a computer, it makes your own copy of this guide in the guides folder, with the real paths of that computer and that user.

💡 To get a folder's path: in File Explorer, hold Shift, right-click the folder, choose Copy as path, then paste it after cd and a space (right-click pastes in PowerShell).

the line in PowerShell now starts with the folder's path, for example PS C:\...\campaign>.

Part 3: every day after that ☀️

☀️Every dayOpen Claude in the campaign folder and type /pfe-inbox, then /pfe-send-held, /pfe-checker and /pfe-applier. The Checker asks how many rows to do: pick a number or ALL, there is no maximum. The Applier first asks how to apply: tick Email, Portal or both. If you tick Portal, it stops and shows how many opportunities wait on each portal, and you pick one, several or all of them. Then say how many of each (a number or ALL). Only applications really sent or submitted count, so it keeps going until it reaches your number. /pfe-hunter finds every opportunity in a country when you need more. It looks at companies and startups first, and still checks labs and universities. After every row the helper updates your tracker, so if it stops halfway (Claude usage used up), just run the same command again later.
📬When a report says "Decisions waiting"A company wrote about an interview, an offer, money or a visa. Open PowerShell, type cd "{{WORKSPACE}}", then claude, then /pfe-send, and choose A, B or C.
📋Once a week (30 minutes)Open campaign\02_tracker\blocked.csv. These are applications the helper cannot do alone (LinkedIn Easy Apply, websites with passwords, a question only you can answer). Do them yourself, then tell Claude "PFE-0123 submitted".
🎤You got an interview!Type /pfe-interview-prep followed by the email address of the person, and Claude prepares everything you need to know: full answers to say out loud, and a page for every technique (yours and the company's) with a little drawing of its graph and what is on each axis. Each interview gets its own folder inside the interviews folder next to campaign, named with the date, the organisation and the person. In it: the prep PDF, a short slide deck to present yourself (the same slides as the model deck you edited; in the notes, first what the slide shows in simple words, and the extra details between // and //, only for questions), and a NotebookLM prompt saved as a simple text note (NOTEBOOKLM_PROMPT.txt, it opens in Notepad). Upload the PDF and the slides to NotebookLM, paste the prompt, and it coaches you in the language of the interview, then asks you practice questions one at a time. A result you chose to leave out is never in the prep or the slides: you talk about your own results instead.

The helper sends at most 50 emails a day, never sends anything about interviews, offers, money or visas without you, and never applies twice to the same person.

If a lab says "we can host you but we cannot pay", or offers very little, the helper never says no for you and never says you will pay for yourself. It looks up what interns in that job usually earn, then writes polite drafts that ask for a fair monthly amount, for example by saying the visa needs proof of about 600 euros a month, and suggests other help such as housing or a grant.

Where to look without typing anything

  • campaign → 05_reports: one report per job. The email report is inbox_<date>.md.
  • campaign → 02_tracker → tracker.xlsx: all your internships in Excel. The first sheet, next_actions, is your to-do list: every thing only you can do (pick a reply, prepare a paper, ask your school for the internship agreement, prepare an interview, a visa step), with its due date. Each run adds new ones and ticks off the ones that are done. The waiting_list sheet shows every application with no reply yet: waiting, follow_up when a reminder is due, or followed_up once it is sent. /pfe-inbox sends the reminders from there. If you applied on a website and there is no email address, nothing is sent and it goes on your to-do list. If someone answers and gives you the name and email of another person to write to, /pfe-inbox adds that person, and /pfe-applier writes to them first and says who suggested it. The next sheet, dashboard, shows pie charts for all rows and for each of the last three weeks. The interviews sheet lists every interview, with the language it will be held in next to the interviewer's name. The offers sheet lists every internship offered to you: who, where, what, the dates, the money, the visa help, where you are now (for example questions asked, accepted) and what happens next. It is updated every time you run /pfe-inbox.
  • campaign → 02_tracker → blocked.csv: the ones you do yourself.
